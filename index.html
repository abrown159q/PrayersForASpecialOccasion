Christian sign of the cross

Lord God, Creator, Redeemer, and Sanctifier, we, with meekness, rely on you for everything. Let us be so blessed as to only see you and your salvation, a gift freely offered, and that will be enough for us. And yet we ask for more, (mention your intentions here...), if it be in your divine will, for we know you are generous and merciful.

Day 4

Christian sign of the cross

God the Holy Spirit, source of Life and Love, sent down to guide us to all truth, open our hearts that we may listen to your voice. Teach us to pray as we ought, knowing that we cannot even ask without your loving guidance. In this, we humbly request (mention your intentions here...) and we know that you will bring to us that which gives us true joy and peace that can only come from you.

Christian sign of the cross

You are the Way, the Truth, and the Life. You carry our burdens, you ask us to take up our cross daily, but you are always there with us. We give everything to you, especially (mention your intentions here...), and know that if we ask anything of you in your name, you will do it.

Day 2

Christian sign of the cross

God our Father, you know our hearts and our deepest desires better than we ourselves know. Guide us to love you more and more each day, realizing that if we trust our desires to you, we will never be disappointed. We especially ask for (mention your intentions here...), which itself, we pray, is good, but it pales in comparison to you.
