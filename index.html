Nine days of Christian Prayer for a Special Occasion

Thank you for joining in praying for our special intentions!

Day 1

Christian sign of the cross

Lord, we love you and you are above all things, deserving all glory and adoration. We are sorry for the ways we have fallen and not lived as is best. We give you thanks for all your gifts and blessings, that even in times of trial you are by our side. We ask for you grace and support, especially for (mention your intentions here...), and trust that you will bring all good from our prayers. Thank you for our friends and family, who show your love for us, and help us to love them as you love us.
