Day 2

Christian sign of the cross

God our Father, you know our hearts and our deepest desires better than we ourselves know. Guide us to love you more and more each day, realizing that if we trust our desires to you, we will never be disappointed. We especially ask for (mention your intentions here...), which itself, we pray, is good, but it pales in comparison to you. We give you glory and know that we do not deserve your love, but that we are worth it as we are wonderfully made in your image and likeness.

We give you thanks for all your gifts and blessings, that even in times of trial you are by our side. We ask for you grace and support, especially for (mention your intentions here...), and trust that you will bring all good from our prayers.
